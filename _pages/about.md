I am a mathematician and data scientist. 

I obtained my PhD under [J.F. Jardine](https://www.math.uwo.ca/faculty/jardine/index.html) in 2018 in higher category theory. Since then I have held postdoctoral
positions at the [University of Haifa](https://mathematics.haifa.ac.il/?page_id=34) and the [University of Bologna](https://matematica.unibo.it/it) mainly focusing on 
category theory and its applications. During my postdoctoral positions, I developed an increasing interest in machine learning. This website describes my projects in machine learning and my research
in pure mathematics.
