Stock and flow models are an important technique in modern epidemiological modelling. [Recent work by Baez Osgood et. al](https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~eptcs/paper.cgi?ACT2022.5) has developed a categorical approach 
to modeling stock and flow models, which was later implemented in the Topos Institute's AlgebraicJulia [here](https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/StockFlow.jl). 

The purpose of this project was to describe categorically how stock and flow models can compose to form larger ones, with a view towards future implementation in AlgebraicJulia. 
An arxiv version of the paper can be found [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02136). This was the subject of a poster presentation at ACT2023, 
available [here](https://act2023.github.io/papers.html).
